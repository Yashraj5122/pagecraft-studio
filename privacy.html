This policy describes this website and Pagecraft Studio’s intended Pinterest publishing integration. The studio is operated by Yashraj in India. Contact: yashrajss5122@gmail.com.

This is a static informational website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact forms, checkout or mailing-list signup. Product links on this website lead to our Gumroad storefront. Gumroad handles product pages, checkout, payments and digital delivery under its own privacy policy. If you purchase, download or subscribe on Gumroad, Gumroad and Pagecraft Studio may receive information needed to fulfill your request and support you. Review Gumroad’s notices before submitting information.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your address, message and information you choose to include. We use this to respond and handle your request. Our email provider also processes those communications. Do not send passwords or sensitive account credentials.

Pinterest integration

We are setting up a private, self-hosted Postiz integration for our own Pinterest business account. It is not a public account-connection service. Once authorized, it requests access to read our account profile, boards and Pins, create or manage boards and Pins, and retrieve available performance data. It uses these permissions to select boards, prepare, schedule and publish our own content and review its performance. An AI assistant helps prepare content and operate the publishing workflow at the owner’s direction.

Storage and sharing

Postiz stores authorized account details, access and refresh tokens, media, and publishing records on our self-hosted server. Access is restricted to authorized operators; administration is accessible through a private VPN. We do not sell Pinterest account data. Relevant information is sent to Pinterest to perform authorized actions. Content and operational information may be processed by our configured AI service providers when we use assistant tools; we do not intentionally include access tokens in prompts. Pinterest’s own service is governed by Pinterest’s privacy policy.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Connected-account credentials are kept while the connection is in use. You can revoke app authorization in Pinterest’s settings. To request deletion of information held by Pagecraft Studio, email the contact above. We will verify the request as appropriate and delete information no longer needed, subject to legal obligations and backup retention. Revoking authorization or deleting local data does not automatically delete Pins already published on Pinterest; manage those in Pinterest separately.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or withdraw authorization. This site and integration are not directed to children. We may update this policy as the studio’s services change and will update the effective date.
